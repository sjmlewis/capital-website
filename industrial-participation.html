UK industrial participation (offset)

The UK Government has said it will introduce a new offsets regime, subject to consultation, so that when it buys defence equipment from abroad, British jobs and benefits are created.

What this means for overseas suppliers

  • UK partners are needed early, often before a bid is submitted.
  • Commitments are usually met over many years, so partners must be stable and capable.
  • Credit is likely to favour real, lasting value: new UK suppliers, new capability, certifications and skilled jobs, especially in smaller companies.
Policy detail is still being finalised. Thresholds, percentages and timescales will be set out by the Ministry of Defence. Capital will update this page when they are published.

Two ways to create UK value

Buy from capable UK makers now

Place work with UK companies that already make what you need, creating UK exports from day one.

Build new UK capability

Transfer technology, support certification or help a smaller company enter defence, creating capability that lasts beyond the contract.

How Capital helps

The platform finds candidates for both routes and highlights the opportunities, such as a smaller company that could gain a certification or a commercial manufacturer ready to enter defence. Your team then decides where to engage.
