Offset alerts

A weekly round-up of new defence sales around the world above the Offset thresholds, and changes to the Offset rules. Each summary is written by Capital from public reports.

Newest first. Checked weekly. Last updated 19 October 2026.

Week of 19 October 20269 alerts

  • Norway16 October 2026

    KNDS and RITEK open a factory in Norway to build 37 Leopard 2A8 tanks

    KNDS and its Norwegian partner RITEK have opened a tank factory at Levanger, which will build 37 of the Norwegian Army's 54 Leopard 2A8NO tanks, with production due to start this autumn. KNDS builds the other 17.

  • Malaysia15 October 2026

    Malaysia approves medium- and short-range air defence purchases under the 13th Malaysia Plan

    Malaysia's defence minister has confirmed approval of a medium-range air defence system (two batteries) for the Air Force and a short-range system for the Army. No system has been chosen; acceptance letters are expected around 2027, and an indicative figure of about US$450–500 million has been reported for the medium-range system.

  • India15 October 2026

    India signs Rs 4,106 crore of contracts for a military satellite and Varunastra torpedoes

    India's Ministry of Defence has signed contracts with New Space India Limited for the GSAT-7C communications satellite for the Air Force (Rs 2,563 crore) and with Bharat Dynamics for Varunastra torpedoes for the Navy (Rs 1,543 crore). Both suppliers are Indian, so India's defence Offset rules do not apply.

  • Thailand14 October 2026

    Thailand moves ahead with the second phase of its Gripen E/F fighter purchase

    The Royal Thai Air Force chief has confirmed plans for the second phase of Thailand's Gripen E/F purchase, part of 12 aircraft planned over three phases, with funding expected in the 2027 budget. Saab's offer includes a long-term Offset package with technology transfer and industrial cooperation.

  • Saudi Arabia Civil13 October 2026

    Riyadh awards Siemens and Alstom about SAR 5.5bn for 94 new metro trains

    The Royal Commission for Riyadh City has signed contracts with Siemens and Alstom worth about SAR 5.5 billion (about US$1.47 billion) for 94 new Riyadh Metro trains with systems, maintenance and training. Deliveries are due to begin in 2028.

  • Colombia12 October 2026

    Colombia reviews the Offset package in its Gripen fighter contract

    Colombia's government is reviewing the Offset projects tied to its contract for 17 Saab Gripen E/F fighters, worth about €3.1 billion, without halting the purchase. It is considering turning projects in solar power, water and health into technology transfer and new capabilities for its air force.

  • United Kingdom Rules2 October 2026

    UK minister says an industry consultation on Offset policy details will start shortly

    Defence minister Luke Pollard has said a consultation with industry to refine the details of the UK's new Offset policy will start shortly. No date or threshold has been given.

  • Austria28 August 2026

    Austria plans a €2.5bn medium-range air defence purchase, aiming to sign by the end of 2026

    Austria plans to buy a medium-range air defence system, two batteries with training and logistics, for about €2.5 billion, and hopes to sign by the end of 2026. No system has been chosen and about six suppliers remain possible; officials have stressed industrial cooperation.

  • Austria20 August 2026

    Austrian Armed Forces open a tender for long-range anti-tank guided missiles

    The Austrian Armed Forces have tendered for long-range anti-tank guided missiles, with €36.2 million in the 2026 budget and €5.4 million in 2027. Applications closed on 2 October, and at least three applicants are to go through to the second phase.

Week of 12 October 202621 alerts

  • Israel14 October 2026

    Israel signs for 12 AH-64E Apache helicopters worth about US$1bn

    Israel's Ministry of Defense has signed an agreement with the Pentagon to buy 12 Boeing AH-64E Apache attack helicopters, worth about US$1 billion, with deliveries due early next decade. The government moved NIS 1 billion into the defence budget to pay for the deal.

  • Bulgaria14 October 2026

    Bulgaria approves a deal with Germany for five more IRIS-T SLM air defence systems

    Bulgaria's Council of Ministers has approved a draft programme agreement with Germany for five more IRIS-T SLM surface-to-air missile systems from Diehl Defence, funded under the EU's SAFE defence loans. The agreement still needs ratification by the National Assembly.

  • Greece14 October 2026

    Greece asks the US to sell it Saronic Corsair uncrewed surface vessels

    Greece has sent the US a Letter of Request to buy Corsair uncrewed surface vessels from Saronic through Foreign Military Sales, after Hellenic Navy trials. The ministry did not give a number; Greek press reports a plan for at least 30 boats.

  • Malaysia Rules14 October 2026

    Malaysia's Offset cap on CPTPP-covered purchases drops from 60% to 40% on 29 November

    Under Malaysia's schedule to the CPTPP trade pact, Offset credits on purchases by the covered ministries are capped at 60% of contract value until 28 November 2026, then 40% until 2030 and 20% until 2034, with none after that.

  • Poland13 October 2026

    Sweden signs contracts to train Polish submarine crews and lease Poland a submarine

    Sweden's procurement agency FMV and the Swedish Navy have signed contracts under Poland's Orka programme to train Polish crews and lease HMS Södermanland to Poland until new boats arrive. They build on Poland's agreement for three Saab A26 submarines, the first due in 2031.

  • United Kingdom Rules13 October 2026

    Still no government response on the UK's defence Offsets consultation

    The Ministry of Defence's written consultation on a possible UK defence Offsets policy closed on 23 December 2025, and as of 13 October 2026 no outcome has been published.

  • Kuwait9 October 2026

    Kuwait buys a £370m British air defence system from MARSS

    Kuwait is buying an integrated air defence system of jammers, radars and interceptors, built around MARSS's NiDAR command and control centre, in a £370 million deal the UK government says will create 100 UK jobs. Kuwait's Offset programme has been suspended since 2015, so no Offset applies.

  • Greece9 October 2026

    Greece agrees about US$4bn of US deals, including 37 more F-16 Block 70 upgrades

    During the US Secretary of State's visit to Athens, Greece agreed to about US$4 billion of US weapons and equipment: about US$1 billion to upgrade 37 F-16 Block 50 jets to Block 70, US$1.2 billion of air weapons and US$750 million of MH-60 helicopter support.

  • Malaysia Civil9 October 2026

    Malaysia's Budget 2027 includes 42 new trains and a RM3.4bn rail overhaul

    Presenting Budget 2027, Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim said the government will buy 42 new train sets for the ETS and KTM Komuter services, and that Prasarana will invest more than RM3.4 billion to rehabilitate its rail services.

  • Denmark9 October 2026

    Denmark takes delivery of the first of four new air defence systems

    Denmark's defence procurement agency has received the first of four new ground-based air defence systems, a NASAMS, from a DKK 58.2 billion programme. Contracts for support equipment are still to be signed.

  • United Arab Emirates7 October 2026

    US approves a possible US$1.04bn sale of APKWS-II guidance sections to the UAE

    The US State Department has approved a possible sale to the United Arab Emirates of up to 10,000 APKWS-II laser guidance sections and related equipment, estimated at US$1.041 billion.

  • Romania7 October 2026

    Romania orders 12 Airbus H225M helicopters under the EU's SAFE defence loans

    Airbus has announced an order from Romania for 12 H225M helicopters, placed through France's armaments agency and paid for with the EU's SAFE defence loans, with Romania's IAR as Airbus's long-term partner. SAFE-funded purchases are exempt from Romania's Offset rules, so this order carries no Romanian Offset.

  • Peru5 October 2026

    Peru moves to fund a second batch of 12 F-16 Block 70 fighters

    Peru's government has asked Congress for extra funding to buy a second batch of 12 F-16 Block 70 fighters from Lockheed Martin, which would take its fleet to 24. In August, Lockheed Martin announced a US$1.8 billion industrial and social collaboration package for Peru's F-16 programme.

  • Austria5 October 2026

    Seven fighter types in the running to replace Austria's early Eurofighters

    Seven fighter types, including Boeing's F-15EX, are reported to be in the running to replace the Austrian Air Force's Tranche 1 Eurofighters.

  • Colombia4 October 2026

    Colombian Army opens a US$34.5m tender for light transport aircraft

    The Colombian Army has opened a tender worth about US$34.5 million for two light transport aircraft to replace three retired C-212s, with offers due on 26 October. The Czech-built L-410 NG is seen as the leading candidate.

  • India2 October 2026

    India's Air Force seeks proposals for 60 medium transport aircraft

    The Indian Air Force's request for proposals for 60 medium transport aircraft, to replace its An-32 and Il-76 fleets, has responses due in December and asks for at least 40% Indian content. Embraer with Mahindra (C-390) and Lockheed Martin with Tata (C-130J-30) are the main bidders.

  • Belgium29 September 2026

    Belgium buys 10 NASAMS air defence firing units from Kongsberg for about €918m

    Kongsberg has signed a contract worth NOK 10 billion (about €918 million) with the Dutch procurement agency, acting for Belgium, for 10 NASAMS firing units. They are part of a Belgian air defence package of about €3.1 billion.

  • Thailand18 September 2026

    Thailand picks Hanwha Ocean to build its next frigate, with a THB 7.25bn Offset package

    The Royal Thai Navy has selected Hanwha Ocean to build a 4,000-tonne frigate for THB 16.73 billion. The Offset package is worth about THB 7.25 billion (43% of the project), with at least 20% of the ship's structure built in Thailand and transfer of designs and combat-system know-how.

  • South Korea15 September 2026

    South Korea approves a US$2.4bn purchase of 20 more MH-60R Seahawk helicopters

    South Korea's Defense Acquisition Program Administration has approved buying about 20 more Lockheed Martin MH-60R Seahawk helicopters for KRW 3.24 trillion (about US$2.4 billion), to be acquired through 2032.

  • Croatia10 September 2026

    Croatia signs €435m contracts for 18 K239 Chunmoo rocket launchers

    Croatia has signed contracts worth €435.2 million (excluding VAT) with Hanwha Aerospace for 18 K239 Chunmoo multiple rocket launchers with ammunition and training, plus a command system from Poland's WB Electronics. The defence minister said Croatian firms, including Končar, will take part.

  • Greece8 September 2026

    Greece signs for two Italian FREMM frigates, with work for Greek industry

    Greece and Italy have signed the arrangement transferring the frigates Carlo Bergamini and Virginio Fasan to the Hellenic Navy in 2028 and 2029, with Greek industry taking part in their maintenance and in integrating new systems.
