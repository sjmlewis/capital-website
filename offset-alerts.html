Offset alerts

Defence programmes around the world that carry, or are likely to carry, offset and industrial participation requirements. Each summary is written by Capital and links to the original source.

Recent alerts

Newest first. Updated weekly. Last updated 13 October 2026.

  • CanadaSeptember 2026

    Canada takes next step with Saab on GlobalEye early-warning aircraft (opens the source in a new tab)

    Canada is moving ahead with Saab, its preferred supplier, towards a contract for six GlobalEye airborne early warning and control aircraft. Defence purchases of this size normally carry obligations under Canada's Industrial and Technological Benefits (ITB) policy.

    Source: Government of Canada↗ (opens in a new tab)

  • NorwaySeptember 2026

    US approves possible sale of 21 Black Hawk helicopters to Norway (opens the source in a new tab)

    The US has notified a possible sale of 21 UH-60M Black Hawk helicopters to Norway, valued at about US$2.3 billion. Norway's industrial cooperation rules normally apply to foreign defence purchases of this size.

    Source: US Federal Register↗ (opens in a new tab)

  • PolandAugust 2026

    Boeing signs Apache offset agreements with Polish industry (opens the source in a new tab)

    Boeing has signed offset agreements with the Polish companies WZL-1 and WCBKT covering training and sustainment for Poland's AH-64E Apache fleet.

    Source: Boeing↗ (opens in a new tab)

  • Saudi ArabiaAugust 2026

    US approves possible APKWS II laser-guided rocket sale to Saudi Arabia (opens the source in a new tab)

    A possible sale of APKWS II laser guidance kits to Saudi Arabia, valued at about US$1.96 billion, has been notified. Saudi Arabia's industrial participation programme, run by GAMI, aims to localise a growing share of defence spending.

    Source: US Federal Register↗ (opens in a new tab)

  • NorwayAugust 2026

    US approves possible sale of 155mm artillery shells to Norway (opens the source in a new tab)

    A possible sale of 155mm M795 high-explosive shells to Norway, valued at about US$270 million, has been notified.

    Source: US Federal Register↗ (opens in a new tab)

  • CanadaJuly 2026

    TKMS chosen as preferred bidder for Canada's new submarines (opens the source in a new tab)

    Canada has selected Germany's TKMS as preferred bidder for up to 12 Type 212CD submarines under the Canadian Patrol Submarine Project. Contract negotiations, including industrial benefits for Canada, now follow.

    Source: Naval News↗ (opens in a new tab)

  • SwitzerlandJuly 2026

    Swiss F-35A offset programme expected to exceed its targets (opens the source in a new tab)

    The Swiss government reports further offset projects placed with Swiss industry under the F-35A purchase, and expects the agreed offset targets to be clearly exceeded.

    Source: Swiss Federal Administration↗ (opens in a new tab)

  • CanadaJune 2026

    Canada and Australia partner on Arctic over-the-horizon radar (opens the source in a new tab)

    Canada has agreed a partnership with Australia to deliver the Arctic Over-the-Horizon Radar, part of Canada's plan to modernise NORAD.

    Source: Department of National Defence, Canada↗ (opens in a new tab)

  • SwitzerlandMarch 2026

    Swiss Armaments Programme 2026 includes more IRIS-T SLM air defence (opens the source in a new tab)

    The Federal Council's Armaments Programme 2026 asks Parliament to fund additional IRIS-T SLM ground-based air defence systems. Swiss offset rules apply to defence purchases from foreign suppliers.

    Source: Swiss Armed Forces (in German)↗ (opens in a new tab)
