Offset alerts

A weekly round-up of new defence sales around the world above the Offset thresholds, and changes to the Offset rules. Each summary is written by Capital from public reports.

Recent alerts

Newest first. Checked weekly. Last updated 9 November 2026.

Week of 9 November 202610 alerts

  • South Korea6 November 2026

    South Korea selects the Sikorsky S-92A+ for its new VIP command helicopters

    South Korea's Defense Acquisition Program Promotion Committee chose the Sikorsky S-92A+ on 6 November for its next command helicopter programme, with a total cost of about KRW 873 billion. Four helicopters will replace older Air Force aircraft by 2031, and the plan includes modification and maintenance technology and parts exports for Korean small firms.

  • Mexico6 November 2026

    US approves a possible US$1.075bn sale of 11 UH-60M Black Hawk helicopters to Mexico

    The US State Department has approved a possible sale to Mexico of 11 Sikorsky UH-60M Black Hawk helicopters with spare engines, navigation systems, radios, training and support, estimated at US$1.075 billion.

  • Sweden6 November 2026

    Saab wins about SEK 2bn from Naval Group to equip Sweden's four new FDI frigates

    Naval Group has ordered weapon control systems and sensors worth about SEK 2 billion from Saab for the four FDI frigates it is building for the Royal Swedish Navy under a contract of about €4.3 billion signed on 31 August. Sweden does not have Offset rules.

  • Denmark6 November 2026

    Denmark and Lithuania join Finland-led joint purchase of Blocker naval mines

    Denmark and Lithuania have confirmed they are buying Forcit Defence's Blocker naval mines through the five-nation Naval Mines Cooperation, under a framework contract Finland signed in June for the group. Finland has described the contract as worth hundreds of millions of euros; no figure per country has been published.

  • Philippines5 November 2026

    India reported in final talks to sell the Philippines more BrahMos missile systems

    India is reported to be in final-stage talks to expand its BrahMos supersonic cruise missile sale to the Philippines, covering about five more systems and nearly 60 missiles, which Indian sources value at US$700–800 million. No deal has yet been announced.

  • Malaysia Civil5 November 2026

    Penang Mutiara Line LRT: RM2.69bn second civil contract awarded

    Malaysia Rapid Transit Corporation has awarded the RM2.69 billion second civil main contract for the Penang Mutiara Line light rail to a joint venture of China Communications Construction Company, TRC Construction and Ufuk Semarak. It covers about 5.5 km of line, including a 3.05 km sea-crossing bridge to Butterworth.

  • United Kingdom Rules4 November 2026

    UK Offset details expected at the Defence Investment Summit on 1 December

    Details of how obligations under the UK's new Offset policy will be assessed, measured and enforced are expected at the Defence Investment Summit on 1 December. The policy is due to apply from April 2027 to contracts of £50 million or more, with each case considered individually.

  • Peru2 November 2026

    Peru awards Leonardo €40.4m to support its four C-27J Spartan aircraft

    Peru's armed forces procurement agency, ACFFAA, has awarded Leonardo a €40.4 million integrated support contract, as sole supplier, for the Peruvian Air Force's four C-27J Spartan transport aircraft. Details of any Offset on this award have not been published.

  • Peru27 October 2026

    Peru seeks to speed up a purchase of 12 search and rescue helicopters, worth up to US$240m

    Peru's government is trying to speed up the purchase of 12 search and rescue helicopters, six before the end of 2026 and six in 2027, at an estimated cost of up to US$240 million. Funding has not yet been allocated; the Leonardo AW139M and Airbus H145M are seen as leading candidates.

  • South Africa CivilOctober 2026

    Wabtec agrees a US$390m support deal for Transnet's D43 and D44 locomotives

    Wabtec has agreed a multi-year contract worth US$390 million with state-owned Transnet Engineering for components, engineering advice and condition-monitoring software for its Class D43 and D44 freight locomotives, with localisation and training included.

Week of 2 November 20268 alerts

  • India1 November 2026

    India reported close to a deal for six converted Boeing 767 tankers from IAI

    India is reported to be nearing an agreement with Israel Aerospace Industries, working with Hindustan Aeronautics, for six converted Boeing 767 tanker aircraft for the Indian Air Force, with a reference value of US$900 million to US$1.1 billion. Approval by the Cabinet Committee on Security is the next step before signature.

  • United Kingdom Rules30 October 2026

    UK publishes its Offset policy: £50m default threshold, applied from April 2027

    The UK Government has published its Offset policy. From April 2027, overseas defence suppliers winning selected contracts worth £50 million or more will normally be expected to reinvest up to the full contract value in the UK, agreed and monitored by a new Offset Authority.

  • India30 October 2026

    India approves eight next-generation corvettes for its Navy, worth over Rs400bn

    India's Cabinet Committee on Security is reported to have approved eight next-generation corvettes for the Indian Navy, worth more than Rs400 billion (about US$4.2 billion), to be built by Garden Reach Shipbuilders & Engineers and Goa Shipyard. As the ships are built by Indian shipyards, India's Offset rules do not apply.

  • Switzerland28 October 2026

    Switzerland shortlists Barak MX and SAMP/T NG for a longer-range air defence system

    Switzerland's Federal Council has decided to buy an additional longer-range ground-based air defence system alongside Patriot, with Israel's Barak MX and France's SAMP/T NG still in the running. The Patriot purchase continues as planned.

  • Poland28 October 2026

    Poland chosen to host Europe's PAC-3 missile servicing centre, worth about PLN 1bn

    Poland has been chosen to host a European servicing centre for the PAC-3 missiles used in Patriot air defence systems, the only one outside the US, at a cost of about PLN 1 billion (about €227 million). Negotiations with Lockheed Martin continue.

  • India26 October 2026

    India gives first approval to buy 24 VVIP helicopters, estimated at about Rs10,000 crore

    India's Defence Acquisition Council gave initial approval on 26 October for 24 Special Utility helicopters for VVIP transport, part of about Rs2.12 lakh crore of approvals that day. The estimated value is about Rs10,000 crore; no supplier has been chosen.

  • Bulgaria26 October 2026

    US Army awards US$48m for chemical reconnaissance sensors on Bulgaria's Strykers

    The US Army has awarded Teledyne FLIR Defense US$48 million to supply nuclear, biological and chemical reconnaissance sensor suites for 10 of the Stryker vehicles Bulgaria is buying, under a Foreign Military Sales arrangement.

  • Denmark21 October 2026

    Naval Group signs three Danish partners for its bid for Denmark's new frigates

    Naval Group has signed memoranda of understanding with Copenhagen Global, Saab Danmark and SH Defence to support its FDI frigate offer for Denmark's future frigate programme, covering drones, communications and mission modules. Denmark has not yet chosen a supplier.

Week of 26 October 20269 alerts

  • Poland22 October 2026

    Kongsberg wins about NOK 700m to supply weapon stations for Poland's K2 tanks

    Kongsberg has signed a contract worth about NOK 700 million with Hyundai Rotem to supply Protector RS4 remote weapon stations for the K2 tanks, recovery vehicles and engineering vehicles ordered by Poland, with a support package. The first systems are due to ship in spring 2027.

  • Morocco21 October 2026

    Morocco in advanced talks to buy the David's Sling air defence system

    Morocco's defence ministry is reported to be at a very advanced stage of negotiations with Rafael and Raytheon for the David's Sling system, including Stunner interceptors, radars, launchers, training and maintenance. No contract value has been published; Rabat is reported to be seeking industrial returns.

  • Netherlands21 October 2026

    US approves a possible US$294m sale of aircraft self-protection systems to the Netherlands

    The US State Department has approved a possible sale to the Netherlands of 16 CIRCM infrared countermeasure systems and 38 radar warning receivers, with support, estimated at US$294 million. Northrop Grumman is the principal contractor.

  • Norway20 October 2026

    Norway sets out its plan to buy up to 20 standardised vessels with the UK and Lithuania

    Norway's Defence Materiel Agency has told industry how it will buy up to 20 standardised vessels: up to 14 naval vessels for Norway, the UK and Lithuania, and up to 6 Coast Guard vessels, under two parallel contracts that one bidder cannot both win. Prequalification comes next, with award of the large-vessel contract targeted for 2027 and first delivery in 2030.

  • Denmark20 October 2026

    Denmark orders more Naval Strike Missiles from Kongsberg for NOK 400m

    Denmark has ordered a further batch of Kongsberg's Naval Strike Missiles, worth NOK 400 million (about US$42 million), in a government-to-government deal through Norway's Defence Materiel Agency. The number of missiles has not been disclosed.

  • Romania20 October 2026

    Romania signs a €920m contract for four naval ships funded by EU SAFE loans

    Romania's government has signed a €920 million contract for two 96-metre offshore patrol vessels and two diving support vessels, to be built at the Mangalia shipyard bought by a Rheinmetall and MSC consortium, with the programme due to finish by 2030. Because it is funded through the EU's SAFE loans, the purchase is exempt from Romania's Offset rules.

  • Greece Rules20 October 2026

    Greek bill would set a minimum 25% national participation in armament programmes

    A Greek defence bill due to go to cabinet and then parliament would set a minimum 25% share for Greek industry in armament programmes. The text has not yet been published, so what will count towards the share is not yet known.

  • Australia13 October 2026

    Australia seeks industry offers for a medium-range ground-based air defence system

    Australia's Department of Defence has issued an industry solicitation for Land 6504, a medium-range ground-based air defence system for the Army, with vendor selection due by the end of 2027. Australia has no Offset policy; its Australian Industry Capability rules apply instead.

  • Saudi Arabia Rules4 September 2026

    Saudi Arabia's new Government Procurement Law takes effect on 2 January 2027

    Saudi Arabia's new Government Procurement Law, published on 4 September 2026, takes effect on 2 January 2027. It gives the Local Content and Government Procurement Authority a role in planning and bid evaluation, with local content preference rules still to be issued.

Week of 19 October 20263 alerts

  • Norway16 October 2026

    KNDS and RITEK open a factory in Norway to build 37 Leopard 2A8 tanks

    KNDS and its Norwegian partner RITEK have opened a tank factory at Levanger, which will build 37 of the Norwegian Army's 54 Leopard 2A8NO tanks, with production due to start this autumn. KNDS builds the other 17.

  • Malaysia15 October 2026

    Malaysia approves medium- and short-range air defence purchases under the 13th Malaysia Plan

    Malaysia's defence minister has confirmed approval of a medium-range air defence system (two batteries) for the Air Force and a short-range system for the Army. No system has been chosen; acceptance letters are expected around 2027, and an indicative figure of about US$450–500 million has been reported for the medium-range system.

  • India15 October 2026

    India signs Rs 4,106 crore of contracts for a military satellite and Varunastra torpedoes

    India's Ministry of Defence has signed contracts with New Space India Limited for the GSAT-7C communications satellite for the Air Force (Rs 2,563 crore) and with Bharat Dynamics for Varunastra torpedoes for the Navy (Rs 1,543 crore). Both suppliers are Indian, so India's defence Offset rules do not apply.
