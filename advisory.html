Advisory services

Alongside the platform, Capital offers direct advisory support to companies and governments working on Offset and Industrial Participation. Advisory work is contracted separately from any platform subscription.

Services

Industrial Participation strategy

Understanding the obligation, choosing the right mix of activities and shaping a credible plan.

Partner development

Structuring technology transfer, training and co-production arrangements with in-country partners.

Structured export finance

Payment structures and financing arrangements that support capital equipment exports under Offset commitments.

Government advisory

Helping governments design industrial and procurement policies that attract foreign technology and investment.

Sectors

Capital works mainly with companies in aerospace, defence, security and space. Any company with technology or capability of interest to a government customer can benefit from the same approach.

How we work

Engagements usually begin with a short discussion of your goals and target market, followed by a written proposal. Work is carried out under a services agreement, with fees agreed case by case.

Training and the roundtable

Offset practitioner course

A practitioner's masterclass for people new to Offset and Industrial Participation: eight modules, a UK elective and a live negotiation simulation, online over nine weeks or in-house over three days.

See the course outline

UK Industrial Co-operation Roundtable

An informal quarterly meeting in London for UK and international defence companies with UK Industrial Participation commitments, with government officials invited to take part. The first meeting takes place in Q4 2026.
