Offset advisory

Offset advisory work is delivered through Constructive Edge, a global advisory group in which Capital is an equal partner. Capital's other work, on UK partnerships, procurement, bids, contracts and finance, is carried out by Capital directly.

Constructive Edge

Constructive Edge is a global Offset and Industrial Participation advisory group headquartered in Guelph, Ontario, near Toronto, Canada, with partners also based in the United States, Belgium, Australia and the UK. It supports industry programme and Offset commitments worth billions of dollars for major aerospace and defence primes.

For Offset advisory work on an obligation or a programme, please contact Constructive Edge at constructive-edge.com.
