The challenge

The UK expects overseas suppliers to invest in its defence industry. Meeting that expectation means finding UK companies that can genuinely make your parts, develop your technology or support your programme, and finding them early.

The UK Offset Policy, published on 30 October 2026, sets the rules: Ministry of Defence purchases of £50 million or more, an obligation normally equal to the contract value, and written approval for every project before it starts.

  • South Korea6 November 2026

    South Korea selects the Sikorsky S-92A+ for its new VIP command helicopters

    South Korea's Defense Acquisition Program Promotion Committee chose the Sikorsky S-92A+ on 6 November for its next command helicopter programme, with a total cost of about KRW 873 billion. Four helicopters will replace older Air Force aircraft by 2031, and the plan includes modification and maintenance technology and parts exports for Korean small firms.

  • Mexico6 November 2026

    US approves a possible US$1.075bn sale of 11 UH-60M Black Hawk helicopters to Mexico

    The US State Department has approved a possible sale to Mexico of 11 Sikorsky UH-60M Black Hawk helicopters with spare engines, navigation systems, radios, training and support, estimated at US$1.075 billion.

  • Sweden6 November 2026

    Saab wins about SEK 2bn from Naval Group to equip Sweden's four new FDI frigates

    Naval Group has ordered weapon control systems and sensors worth about SEK 2 billion from Saab for the four FDI frigates it is building for the Royal Swedish Navy under a contract of about €4.3 billion signed on 31 August. Sweden does not have Offset rules.

  • Denmark6 November 2026

    Denmark and Lithuania join Finland-led joint purchase of Blocker naval mines

    Denmark and Lithuania have confirmed they are buying Forcit Defence's Blocker naval mines through the five-nation Naval Mines Cooperation, under a framework contract Finland signed in June for the group. Finland has described the contract as worth hundreds of millions of euros; no figure per country has been published.

  • Philippines5 November 2026

    India reported in final talks to sell the Philippines more BrahMos missile systems

    India is reported to be in final-stage talks to expand its BrahMos supersonic cruise missile sale to the Philippines, covering about five more systems and nearly 60 missiles, which Indian sources value at US$700–800 million. No deal has yet been announced.

  • Malaysia Civil5 November 2026

    Penang Mutiara Line LRT: RM2.69bn second civil contract awarded

    Malaysia Rapid Transit Corporation has awarded the RM2.69 billion second civil main contract for the Penang Mutiara Line light rail to a joint venture of China Communications Construction Company, TRC Construction and Ufuk Semarak. It covers about 5.5 km of line, including a 3.05 km sea-crossing bridge to Butterworth.

  • United Kingdom Rules4 November 2026

    UK Offset details expected at the Defence Investment Summit on 1 December

    Details of how obligations under the UK's new Offset policy will be assessed, measured and enforced are expected at the Defence Investment Summit on 1 December. The policy is due to apply from April 2027 to contracts of £50 million or more, with each case considered individually.

  • Peru2 November 2026

    Peru awards Leonardo €40.4m to support its four C-27J Spartan aircraft

    Peru's armed forces procurement agency, ACFFAA, has awarded Leonardo a €40.4 million integrated support contract, as sole supplier, for the Peruvian Air Force's four C-27J Spartan transport aircraft. Details of any Offset on this award have not been published.

  • Peru27 October 2026

    Peru seeks to speed up a purchase of 12 search and rescue helicopters, worth up to US$240m

    Peru's government is trying to speed up the purchase of 12 search and rescue helicopters, six before the end of 2026 and six in 2027, at an estimated cost of up to US$240 million. Funding has not yet been allocated; the Leonardo AW139M and Airbus H145M are seen as leading candidates.
