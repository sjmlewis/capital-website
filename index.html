The challenge

The UK expects overseas suppliers to invest in its defence industry. Meeting that expectation means finding UK companies that can genuinely make your parts, develop your technology or support your programme, and finding them early.

A web search turns up distributors, sales offices and advertisers alongside real manufacturers. Reviewing their capabilities, financial health, compliance and certifications takes a significant investment of time.

Advisory services

How the platform works

Six steps from a parts list to first contact. Your engineers stay in control at every one of them.

  1. Upload your parts list

    Plain, unclassified descriptions. No drawings, part numbers or controlled data are needed.

  2. Choose your priorities

    Put ready-now manufacturers first, or give more weight to the UK value a partnership creates.

  3. Exclude competitors

    Remove competitor groups from your results. Anything excluded stays listed, so you can bring it back.

  4. Review the shortlist

    Results for each line are grouped by readiness, each with a reason and links to the company's own published information.

  5. Check the companies you pick

    Financial health, ownership and security information for the firms you shortlist.

  6. Make first contact in your own name

    A sanitised first-stage capability enquiry for each company, with a tracker for replies.

Built for engineers, not marketing

Every match comes with a reason that is simple to understand, and a link to the company's own published information. Companies that only sell or distribute are kept out of the manufacturer results.

Latest Offset alerts

A weekly round-up of new defence sales around the world above the Offset thresholds, and changes to the Offset rules.

Checked weekly. Last updated 14 October 2026.

See all Offset alerts
  • Israel14 October 2026

    Israel signs for 12 AH-64E Apache helicopters worth about US$1bn

    Israel's Ministry of Defense has signed an agreement with the Pentagon to buy 12 Boeing AH-64E Apache attack helicopters, worth about US$1 billion, with deliveries due early next decade. The government moved NIS 1 billion into the defence budget to pay for the deal.

  • Bulgaria14 October 2026

    Bulgaria approves a deal with Germany for five more IRIS-T SLM air defence systems

    Bulgaria's Council of Ministers has approved a draft programme agreement with Germany for five more IRIS-T SLM surface-to-air missile systems from Diehl Defence, funded under the EU's SAFE defence loans. The agreement still needs ratification by the National Assembly.

  • Greece14 October 2026

    Greece asks the US to sell it Saronic Corsair uncrewed surface vessels

    Greece has sent the US a Letter of Request to buy Corsair uncrewed surface vessels from Saronic through Foreign Military Sales, after Hellenic Navy trials. The ministry did not give a number; Greek press reports a plan for at least 30 boats.

  • Malaysia Rules14 October 2026

    Malaysia's Offset cap on CPTPP-covered purchases drops from 60% to 40% on 29 November

    Under Malaysia's schedule to the CPTPP trade pact, Offset credits on purchases by the covered ministries are capped at 60% of contract value until 28 November 2026, then 40% until 2030 and 20% until 2034, with none after that.

  • Poland13 October 2026

    Sweden signs contracts to train Polish submarine crews and lease Poland a submarine

    Sweden's procurement agency FMV and the Swedish Navy have signed contracts under Poland's Orka programme to train Polish crews and lease HMS Södermanland to Poland until new boats arrive. They build on Poland's agreement for three Saab A26 submarines, the first due in 2031.

  • United Kingdom Rules13 October 2026

    Still no government response on the UK's defence Offsets consultation

    The Ministry of Defence's written consultation on a possible UK defence Offsets policy closed on 23 December 2025, and as of 13 October 2026 no outcome has been published.

  • Kuwait9 October 2026

    Kuwait buys a £370m British air defence system from MARSS

    Kuwait is buying an integrated air defence system of jammers, radars and interceptors, built around MARSS's NiDAR command and control centre, in a £370 million deal the UK government says will create 100 UK jobs. Kuwait's Offset programme has been suspended since 2015, so no Offset applies.

  • Greece9 October 2026

    Greece agrees about US$4bn of US deals, including 37 more F-16 Block 70 upgrades

    During the US Secretary of State's visit to Athens, Greece agreed to about US$4 billion of US weapons and equipment: about US$1 billion to upgrade 37 F-16 Block 50 jets to Block 70, US$1.2 billion of air weapons and US$750 million of MH-60 helicopter support.

  • Malaysia Civil9 October 2026

    Malaysia's Budget 2027 includes 42 new trains and a RM3.4bn rail overhaul

    Presenting Budget 2027, Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim said the government will buy 42 new train sets for the ETS and KTM Komuter services, and that Prasarana will invest more than RM3.4 billion to rehabilitate its rail services.
