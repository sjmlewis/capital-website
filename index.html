The challenge

The UK expects overseas suppliers to invest in its defence industry. Meeting that expectation means finding UK companies that can genuinely make your parts, develop your technology or support your programme, and finding them early.

A web search turns up distributors, sales offices and advertisers alongside real manufacturers. Reviewing their capabilities, financial health, compliance and certifications takes a significant investment of time.

Advisory services

How the platform works

Six steps from a parts list to first contact. Your engineers stay in control at every one of them.

  1. Upload your parts list

    Plain, unclassified descriptions. No drawings, part numbers or controlled data are needed.

  2. Choose your priorities

    Put ready-now manufacturers first, or give more weight to the UK value a partnership creates.

  3. Exclude competitors

    Remove competitor groups from your results. Anything excluded stays listed, so you can bring it back.

  4. Review the shortlist

    Results for each line are grouped by readiness, each with a reason and links to the company's own published information.

  5. Check the companies you pick

    Financial health, ownership and security information for the firms you shortlist.

  6. Make first contact in your own name

    A sanitised first-stage capability enquiry for each company, with a tracker for replies.

Built for engineers, not marketing

Every match comes with a reason that is simple to understand, and a link to the company's own published information. Companies that only sell or distribute are kept out of the manufacturer results.
