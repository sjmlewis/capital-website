About Capital

Capital was founded in 2013 by Stephen Lewis to help companies create lasting value through Offset and Industrial Participation. It now combines that experience with Capital's UK Defence Localisation Platform.

Founder

Stephen Lewis has more than 25 years' experience in Offset, Industrial Participation and cross-border trade finance, first with a leading international consulting and financing organisation and, since 2013, through Capital.

His work has covered structured export finance, technology transfer and training programmes, and advisory work for governments developing their own industrial policies.

Selected experience

Projects Stephen has led or contributed to.

  • TaiwanLinked a client with a UK aerospace company that was outsourcing component manufacture, leading to technology transfer, training, certification and about USD 100m of component exports to the UK over three years.
  • South KoreaHelped three steel manufacturers export more than USD 200m of commercial steel to European and US defence clients under five Offset contracts over six years.
  • IndiaSet up technology cooperation with agencies of the Defence Research and Development Organisation, within international sanctions rules, and built partnerships with Indian aerospace and defence companies for a client's bid.
  • FinlandEstablished a self-funding national R&D technology centre within the main engineering university, for long-term defence product development with Finnish industry and SMEs.
  • South AfricaHelped three local companies win new export sales over three years in ship and oil-platform repair, yellow goods and jewellery, worth about USD 80m in total.
  • RomaniaSecured around EUR 30m of technology products for Romanian universities and research centres, supported by an international defence company.
  • KuwaitNegotiated technological assistance for the aerospace engineering and quality control capability of the Gulf University of Science and Technology.
  • EgyptStructured a counter-purchase and escrow arrangement to fund stage payments for naval vessels.
  • BrazilArranged aircraft finance linked to a US defence client's Offset commitment to the Brazilian Air Force.
  • ThailandTook on a client's obligation, with a penalty for non-performance, to generate USD 20m of additional agricultural exports, and arranged the sales with an overseas buyer.
  • United Arab EmiratesNegotiated a steel off-take agreement through an international trading network, improving the mill's financing.

How Capital works

Offset and Industrial Participation carry commercial and political risk. Capital works on an open, documented basis and complies with the anti-corruption and export-control laws that apply to its work.
